Frequently Asked Questions

Does Sport Checklist require an account?

No. Sport Checklist does not require registration. Your checklists are stored locally on your device.

Can I use Sport Checklist offline?

Yes. The app is designed to work without requiring a permanent internet connection.

Can I customize my checklists?

Yes. You can create your own checklists, categories and items.

Is my data shared?

No. Your checklist data remains on your device.
