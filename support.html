Our Applications

App Checklist includes dedicated checklist applications designed for different needs:

  • Sport Checklist - organize your sports equipment, training sessions and competitions.
  • Trip Checklist - prepare your travels and never forget important items.
  • Shop Checklist - manage your shopping lists quickly and easily.

Common Questions

Where is my checklist data stored?

Your checklist data is stored locally on your device. App Checklist does not require an account and does not upload your checklist content to external servers.

I changed my phone. Can I recover my checklists?

Since checklist data is stored locally, recovery depends on your device backup system. We recommend keeping appropriate backups of your device data.

I found a problem. What should I do?

If you experience a problem, please contact us with:

  • application name (Sport Checklist, Trip Checklist or Shop Checklist)
  • device model
  • Android version
  • description of the problem
  • screenshots if available
