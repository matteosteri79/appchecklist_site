Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how App Checklist handles information when users access our website, Progressive Web Apps (PWAs), and mobile applications.

App Checklist is designed with privacy in mind. Our applications do not require user accounts and checklist data is primarily stored locally on the user's device.

1. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for the processing of personal data related to App Checklist services is the developer of App Checklist.

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, users can contact us through the contact information provided on this website.

2. Legal Basis for Processing

When applicable, personal data processing is carried out in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation - GDPR).

Since App Checklist does not require account registration and does not collect personal information for its core functionality, most application data remains under the user's control on their own device.

3. Information We Collect

App Checklist does not require users to provide personal information such as name, email address, phone number or location in order to use the applications.

We do not sell, rent or share users' personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Stored on Your Device

Checklist items, preferences, themes, language settings and application configurations may be stored locally on the user's device through application storage or browser local storage.

This information is not transmitted to App Checklist servers and remains stored on the user's device.

5. Permissions

App Checklist applications request only permissions that are necessary for providing their functionality.

Currently, the applications do not require access to sensitive information such as contacts, camera, microphone or location.

6. Third-Party Services

App Checklist applications may be distributed through Google Play. Google may process certain technical information related to app installation, updates, security and service availability according to Google's Privacy Policy.

App Checklist does not use third-party advertising networks or user tracking services.

7. Cookies

The App Checklist website does not use advertising cookies or tracking cookies.

Technical cookies or similar technologies may be used only when necessary for the correct operation of the website.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by App Checklist.

Since checklist data is stored locally on the user's device, users are responsible for maintaining appropriate backups of their own data.

9. Children's Privacy

App Checklist services are designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Data Deletion

Since checklist information is stored locally on your device, you can delete your data at any time by removing the application or clearing application data through your device settings.

11. Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws, users may have rights regarding their personal data, including access, correction and deletion requests.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
